NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 – Canton, MI
Project theme: NASA Space Apps Challenge – The Forgotten Machines of Space
Team : Rover Friends 5 Memebers
About the Team
Hi everyone! I’m a young space explorer who loves planets, robots, rockets, and space mysteries. For the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Rover Friends will uncover the stories behind equipment left on the Moon and Mars and discover how these forgotten objects helped scientists explore space. Join us for a fun space adventure!
About the Challenge
Since the 1960s, NASA has left hardware across the solar system–on the Moon, on Mars, and in deep space. From rovers and instruments that completed their science missions to probes still traveling away from Earth this hardware varies widely in both its purpose and current level of functionality. Your challenge is to tell the story of some or all of this equipment that introduces school-age space enthusiasts to the hardware and the science it made possible.
